# 《食南之徒》原创分镜与封面提示词

## 生成方式

- 工具：Codex 内置 imagegen。
- 用途分类：`historical-scene`、`illustration-story`；第 16 组为现代对照插画。
- 输出：18 张 2×2 四格分镜，每格均可独立裁切为竖屏镜头；另生成 1 张跨三种比例安全裁切的原创封面底图。
- 角色锚点：第 1 组确定唐蒙；第 6 组确定甘蔗和甘叶；后续图片使用已落盘图片作为角色与风格参考。

## 全部分镜共用提示

```text
Use case: historical-scene
Asset type: 2x2 storyboard sheet for a vertical Chinese book-commentary video
Period: Western Han dynasty, mission to the Nanyue kingdom around 135 BCE
Style/medium: bright contemporary Chinese editorial illustration; gongbi-inspired clean linework; flat gouache color; subtle warm paper grain; polished narrative art
Composition/framing: exact 2x2 grid; four equal rectangular panels; thin warm-ivory gutters; portrait sheet around 9:16; every panel is a complete crop-safe vertical composition
Main character: Tang Meng, a stocky middle-aged Han Chinese minor official and food lover, round weathered intelligent face, short moustache and small beard, hair bound beneath a simple dark teal Han-era headcloth, teal and warm ochre cross-collar robe; preserve this identity across scenes
Lighting/mood: bright daytime sunlight or generous indoor skylight; high-key exposure; open readable shadows; optimistic visual clarity even when the subject is serious
Color palette: warm ivory, mineral turquoise, celadon green, sky blue, persimmon orange, cinnabar accents, sunlight gold
Period constraints: Western Han and Nanyue architecture, clothing, pottery, lacquerware, bamboo slips and boats; no Tang-dynasty futou; no later imperial costumes; no modern props unless the scene explicitly says modern
Constraints: exactly four panels; no text, letters, numbers, captions, logos, watermarks, book covers, film stills, celebrity or actor likeness
Avoid: low-key lighting, night scenes, near-black backgrounds, muddy brown-gray cast, crushed shadows, heavy vignette, horror mood, extra panels, merged panels
```

## 角色补充

- 甘蔗：约 12 岁的南越女孩，清醒坚韧，黑发简洁束起，浅青绿交领衣与暖橙腰带；不是成人妆容。
- 甘叶：甘蔗的母亲，约 35 岁的王宫厨师，朴素麻布交领衣、围裙与束发，沉静勤劳。
- 赵佗：高龄南越王，银白发须，威严但带乡愁，不采用影视演员形象。
